ADSBee m1421: MLAT timestamps (AT+MLAT)

Bench results, t-0413, 2026-10-13. m1421 on an ADSBee 1421 Programmer, cabled to a signal generator with sample-exact frame start times.

Result. With AT+MLAT=1, 29,903 frames were stamped in all runs and 0 carried a wrong stamp (more than 2 µs off the schedule). Stamp error 38 ns RMS, 95 % within 71 ns. Stamped share of decoded frames: 86 % at 100 frames/s, 60 % at 500, 35 % at 1,000, 12 % at 2,000, 1 % at 4,000. Decode against MLAT off: +0.02 (-0.04 to +0.08) points at 100 frames/s, -0.12 (-0.22 to -0.02) at 500, -0.29 (-0.43 to -0.16) at 1,000, -0.38 (-0.55 to -0.20) at 2,000, -0.13 (-0.37 to +0.15) at 4,000. With the setting off the image decodes like main.

How a packet gets a stamp

  • The LR2021 raises SyncwordValid on DIO5 at a packet's sync event and holds it until it is cleared. A CC1314 timer captures the edge at 48 MHz and uDMA stores the capture, so no interrupt runs per packet.
  • The loop drain sweep clears the latch (one command per packet at most) and, when the FIFO holds exactly one packet, reads the LR2021's own timestamp of its latest sync event.
  • The packet is stamped with the edge time only when it is alone in its FIFO read, one edge is old enough to be surely in that read (145 µs before the level request), and edge and timestamp name the same event (within 1 µs; two events are at least 120 µs apart). The edge is the first event after a clear and the timestamp the latest event of all, so agreement means no other event came in between.
  • Every other packet is marked as having no MLAT timestamp. BEAST and RAW report 0 for it, which MLAT clients skip.
  • With MLAT on, the sweep finishes its short SPI frames in one main-loop pass and starts every 500 µs; it skips the timestamp read for multi-packet reads and the clear in dense traffic.

  • Rollover and gaps: the 24-bit timer wraps every 349.5 ms; the burst spans 4.5 s with 500 ms silences, one clock fit per burst, so a stamp off by a timer period would show as mis-paired.
  • Stalls: console output held the main loop for up to 13 ms during the burst. Time base restarts: 0.
  • SYNC sleep: supply current in sleep 107 µA with MLAT off and 107 µA with MLAT on (median over 3 s). The capture timer is stopped for the sleep and the count restarts ahead of where it stopped: first stamp after the sleep minus last stamp before it was positive in all 2 sleeps.
  • DF17 preamble mode: no packet is stamped by design (0 of 1,024).

Not tested

  • Live sky. The module is cabled to the generator. Real traffic has overlapping frames, Mode A/C and noise detections; each takes an edge or a read slot, so the stamped share will be lower than on the bench at the same decoded rate.
  • Two receivers on one signal, an MLAT server, absolute delay, unit-to-unit spread, temperature, other LR2021 units (the 135 to 140 µs FIFO delay behind the 145 / 125 µs window was measured on one unit).
  • UAT and 1090 on the air at the same instant.

Bench state

Feeds: the m1421 has no network path and reported only to the test script. The 1090U on the same Pi is cabled to a different generator and has Ethernet and Wi-Fi station off. No feed could carry the synthetic frames. Maximum level into the module −20 dBm. The LR2021 never transmitted.
